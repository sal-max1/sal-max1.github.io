A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious about the
stories in the numbers.

I’m an undergraduate at the University of California, Berkeley, studying Economics and Cognitive Science. My work sits at the intersection of data journalism, public policy and political behavior.

As a data reporter at the Daily Californian, I use public records, institutional data and quantitative analysis to investigate questions affecting the Berkeley community. At AAPI Data, I work with Census and federal datasets to research demographic and policy trends and communicate findings through visualizations and data-driven writing.

I’m particularly interested in how data can illuminate systems that are otherwise difficult to see — and how careful reporting can make those systems understandable to readers.
